# KhaanaDa — Customer User Guide

**Version**: 0.1.0
**Last Updated**: 2024-01-16

## Welcome to KhaanaDa! 🍽️

**Cravings Delivered.**

KhaanaDa is Pakistan's food delivery platform. Order from your favorite restaurants and get delicious food delivered to your doorstep.

---

## ⚠️ Important Notice

**This is a development demo.** The current version uses simulated data and does not process real orders or payments. No actual food will be delivered.

This guide describes how to use the application as currently implemented.

---

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Click **"Login"** in the top-right corner
2. Click **"Sign Up"** at the bottom of the form
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Password
4. Click **"Sign Up"**

**Note**: In demo mode, any credentials will work. No email verification is required.

### Logging In

1. Click **"Login"** in the top-right corner
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **"Sign In"**

**Note**: In demo mode, any credentials will work.

### Logging Out

1. Click your profile icon (top-right)
2. Click **"Logout"**

## Finding Restaurants

### Browse All Restaurants

1. Click **"Restaurants"** in the navigation menu
2. Scroll through the list of available restaurants
3. Each restaurant card shows:
   - Restaurant name and image
   - Cuisine type
   - Rating (out of 5 stars)
   - Delivery time
   - Delivery fee
   - Minimum order amount
   - Open/Closed status

### Search for Restaurants

1. Go to the Restaurants page
2. Use the search bar at the top
3. Type the name of a restaurant, cuisine, or dish
4. Results update automatically as you type

**Example searches**:
- "Biryani" — Find biryani restaurants
- "Burger" — Find burger places
- "Lahori" — Find Lahori cuisine

### Filter by Cuisine

1. Go to the Restaurants page
2. Click the **"Filter"** button
3. Select a cuisine category:
   - Pakistani
   - Fast Food
   - Chinese
   - BBQ & Grill
   - Biryani & Rice
   - Pizza
   - Burgers
   - Desserts
   - Beverages
   - Breakfast
   - Seafood
   - Healthy
4. Only restaurants matching the category will display

### Sort Restaurants

1. Go to the Restaurants page
2. Click the **"Filter"** button
3. Choose a sort option:
   - **Rating: High to Low** — Best rated first
   - **Delivery Time** — Fastest delivery first
   - **Price: Low to High** — Cheapest delivery fee first

## Viewing a Restaurant

### Restaurant Details Page

Click on any restaurant to view its details:

1. **Hero Banner**: Large image of the restaurant
2. **Restaurant Info**:
   - Name and cuisine type
   - Rating and review count
   - Delivery time
   - Delivery fee
3. **Tabs**:
   - **Menu**: Browse food items
   - **About**: Restaurant information
   - **Reviews**: Customer reviews

### Menu Tab

The menu shows all available food items:

- **Category Sidebar**: Filter by food category (Main Course, BBQ, Sides, etc.)
- **Food Items**: Each item shows:
  - Image
  - Name and description
  - Price (in PKR)
  - Dietary indicators:
    - 🌿 **Veg** — Vegetarian
    - 🌱 **Vegan** — No animal products
    - 🌶️ **Spicy** — Contains spice
    - ⭐ **Popular** — Customer favorite
  - **Add to Cart** button

### About Tab

View restaurant information:
- Full description
- Address
- Opening hours
- Delivery information
- Rating details

### Reviews Tab

See what other customers say:
- Overall rating
- Individual reviews with:
  - Customer name
  - Star rating
  - Review text
  - Date

## Adding Items to Cart

### Add an Item

1. Browse the restaurant menu
2. Find an item you want
3. Click **"Add to Cart"** button
4. The item is added to your cart
5. Cart badge (top-right) shows item count
6. Cart drawer opens automatically

### Adjust Quantity

**In the cart drawer**:
1. Click the cart icon (top-right)
2. Find the item
3. Click **+** to increase quantity
4. Click **-** to decrease quantity
5. Click **trash icon** to remove item

**On the menu page**:
- After adding an item, the "Add to Cart" button changes to quantity controls
- Use **+** and **-** to adjust
- Item count shows in orange badge

### View Cart

1. Click the cart icon (top-right)
2. Cart drawer slides out from the right
3. See all items in your cart:
   - Item image and name
   - Selected modifiers (if any)
   - Quantity
   - Price per item
   - Total price

### Cart Summary

At the bottom of the cart:
- **Subtotal**: Total for all items
- **Delivery Fee**: Cost of delivery
- **Tax (5% GST)**: Government tax
- **Total**: Final amount to pay

## Placing an Order

### Checkout

1. With items in your cart, click **"Checkout"** button
2. Or click cart icon and click **"Checkout"** link

### Select Delivery Address

1. See your saved addresses
2. Click the address you want to use
3. Addresses show:
   - Label (Home, Office, etc.)
   - Full street address
   - Area and city

**Add New Address**:
- Click **"Add New Address"** (coming soon in demo)

### Select Payment Method

Choose how to pay:

1. **Cash on Delivery (COD)** 💵
   - Pay when your order arrives
   - Most popular in Pakistan
   - No online payment needed

2. **Online Payment** 💳
   - Pay with JazzCash, easypaisa, or bank card
   - **Note**: Not yet implemented in demo

### Review Order

See your order summary:
- All items with quantities
- Subtotal
- Delivery fee
- Tax
- **Total amount**

### Place Order

1. Review all details
2. Click **"Place Order"** button
3. Wait for confirmation

### Order Confirmation

After placing your order:
- ✅ Green checkmark appears
- "Order Confirmed" message
- Order ID (e.g., KD-LX2A4B)
- Total amount
- Payment method
- Buttons:
  - **Track Order** — View order status
  - **Home** — Return to home page

## Tracking Your Order

### View Order Status

1. Click **"Track Order"** after placing order
2. Or go to **"My Orders"** from navigation

### Order Timeline

See your order progress:

1. **Order Placed** ⏰ — Your order is received
2. **Confirmed** ✅ — Restaurant accepted your order
3. **Preparing** 👨‍🍳 — Food is being cooked
4. **Ready for Pickup** 📦 — Order ready for rider
5. **On the Way** 🛵 — Rider has your order
6. **Delivered** 🎉 — Order delivered to you

**Visual Timeline**:
- Completed steps show orange circles
- Current step is highlighted
- Future steps are gray

### Order Details

Each order shows:
- Order ID
- Restaurant name
- Order status (color-coded badge)
- Date and time
- Total amount
- Items ordered
- Estimated delivery time

### Order History

View all your past orders:
1. Click **"My Orders"** in navigation
2. See list of all orders
3. Most recent orders at top
4. Click any order for details

### Reorder

For delivered orders:
1. Go to **"My Orders"**
2. Find a past order
3. Click **"Reorder"** button
4. Items added to cart
5. Proceed to checkout

## Managing Your Profile

### View Profile

1. Click profile icon (top-right)
2. Or click **"Profile"** in navigation

### Edit Profile

1. Go to Profile page
2. Update your information:
   - Full Name
   - Email
   - Phone Number
3. Click **"Save Changes"**

**Note**: In demo mode, changes are not persisted.

### Manage Addresses

1. Go to Profile page
2. Click **"Addresses"** tab
3. See your saved addresses
4. **Add Address**: Click **"Add New Address"** (coming soon)
5. **Delete Address**: Click trash icon next to address
6. **Default Address**: Marked with green "Default" badge

### Change Language

1. Go to Profile page
2. Click **"Language"** tab
3. Select:
   - 🇬🇧 **English**
   - 🇵🇰 **اردو** (Urdu)
4. Entire app switches language
5. Urdu uses RTL (right-to-left) layout

**Quick Language Toggle**:
- Click language icon (globe) in header
- Instantly switches between EN/UR

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Is this a real food delivery service?

**A**: No, this is a development demo. No real orders are processed and no food is delivered.

### Q: How do I create an account?

**A**: Click "Login" → "Sign Up" → Fill in your details → Click "Sign Up". Any credentials work in demo mode.

### Q: Can I pay online?

**A**: Online payment is shown but not implemented. Only Cash on Delivery (COD) works in demo mode.

### Q: Will my order be delivered?

**A**: No, this is a demo. Orders are simulated and not actually processed.

### Q: Can I track my order in real-time?

**A**: No, order tracking is static in demo mode. Real-time tracking requires backend integration.

### Q: Is my data saved?

**A**: No, all data is stored in memory and lost when you refresh the page or close the browser.

### Q: Can I use the app in Urdu?

**A**: Yes! Click the language toggle (globe icon) to switch between English and Urdu.

### Q: What cities do you deliver to?

**A**: Demo restaurants are in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, and Rawalpindi. Real delivery zones require backend configuration.

### Q: How much is the delivery fee?

**A**: Delivery fees vary by restaurant (PKR 50-120 in demo). Real fees depend on distance and restaurant.

### Q: Can I cancel my order?

**A**: In demo mode, cancellation is not implemented. Real cancellation depends on order status and restaurant policy.
